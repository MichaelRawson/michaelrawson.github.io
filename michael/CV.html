Research Interests

I am most interested in the theory and practice of automated theorem proving for classical first-order logic, including machine-learned heuristic methods and proof reconstruction. I have also applied automated reasoning to security, protocol design, verification, and interactive theorem proving.

I develop the world-class automated theorem prover Vampire with an international team. Vampire has seen widespread use in many areas, particularly as automation for interactive systems. Increasingly it is also used standalone in research mathematics, notably algebra.
